# Privacy Policy — Visit Reminders

Last updated: 2026-08-13

Visit Reminders (“the extension”) is a Chrome extension that stores timer reminders on your device.

## What we collect

The extension does **not** operate a backend and does **not** collect analytics, advertising IDs, or account credentials.

On your computer only (`chrome.storage.local`), it may store:

- Reminder identifiers and activity type
- Display names that already appeared on a page you opened (for example a pet name shown on a training status page)
- Observed and estimated deadline timestamps
- Your on/off settings for reminders and notifications

It does not read or store passwords, cookies, session tokens, email addresses, or currency balances.

## How data is used

Local storage is used only to show countdowns in the popup and to schedule local Chrome alarms/notifications.

## Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or transmit this data to a server we control. Notifications are shown by Chrome/your operating system on this device.

## Permissions

- `storage` — local reminder state  
- `alarms` — local deadline scheduling  
- `notifications` — local alerts  

The extension only inspects pages after you navigate to a small list of supported addresses. It does not crawl the web in the background.

## Retention and deletion

Data stays in your browser profile until you remove a reminder, clear site/extension data, or uninstall the extension.

## Contact

Use the Chrome Web Store support email listed on the extension’s store page.
